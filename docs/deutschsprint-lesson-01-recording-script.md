## A1-01-02 — Seite 03: Besondere Buchstaben

Read the following sequence slowly, then repeat it at a natural pace. Give each item a pause.

- Ä — Äpfel — Käse
- Ö — Öl — schön
- Ü — Tür — grün
- Eszett — Straße — Fuß

Check rounded front vowels Ö and Ü, and the voiceless /s/ in Straße and Fuß. Do not pronounce ß as B.

## A1-01-03 — Seite 04: Aussprache

For each row, demonstrate the highlighted **sound**, then pronounce the example(s). Do not merely spell each digraph. The IPA below is a recording direction, not spoken text.

| Pattern | Sound | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ei | /aɪ̯/ | Eis |
| ie | /iː/ | Liebe |
| eu / äu | /ɔʏ̯/ | Eule, Häuser |
| au | /aʊ̯/ | Haus |
| sch | /ʃ/ | Schule |
| ch | /ç/ and /x/ | ich, Buch |
| sp / st | /ʃp/, /ʃt/, contrast /st/ | Sport, Stein, Kiste |
| ph | /f/ | Alphabet |
| z | /ts/ | Zahn |

Repeat the contrast pairs: Eis — Liebe; ich — Buch. Pause between items. No added German sentences or new vocabulary.

## A1-01-04 — Seite 05: Buchstabieren

Two speakers if possible. Read the dialogue once slowly, then once at a natural pace:

**A:** Wie heißen Sie?

**B:** Ich heiße Ahmad.

**A:** Wie schreibt man das?

**B:** A – H – M – A – D.

Finally, spell the two names individually and slowly (letter names, not their sounds in words):

- Ahmad: A – H – M – A – D.
- Mira: M – I – R – A.

## Audio acceptance

- Verify every spoken item against the script and the book.
- Confirm German pronunciation with a proficient German speaker, especially letter names, umlauts, ch, initial sp/st and z, and the isolated letter-name spelling.
- Check the audio for clipping, background noise, and audible silence/pause boundaries. Export a separate mono MP3 for each ID; consistent perceived volume, no music.
- Browser speech practice is optional and is visibly identified as synthesized; it is not the approved recording. Do not set the manifest's `source` to an unverified placeholder.
